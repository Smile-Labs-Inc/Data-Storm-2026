The blind spot in trade spend

We reward yesterday's best sellers — but they're already near full.
  • Top outlets are capped. Limited coolers, shelf space and delivery rounds mean a busy shop can't sell much more — extra spend there is largely wasted.
  • The real growth is invisible. Many outlets sell less than they could simply because they were never supplied or supported to their true ceiling.
  • Sales history hides this. What an outlet sold is a floor on what it can sell — never the full story.

04 · The Framework (a)Smile Labs

From potential to a spending plan

Potential-Based Allocation: every rupee chases the most extra litres it can buy.
  • Fund the gap, not the glory. Spend goes to outlets with the most untapped room to grow — not the ones already at the top.
  • Diminishing returns, respected. Each outlet gets exactly what it can usefully absorb; we stop before money is wasted.
  • Mathematically optimal. The plan provably squeezes the maximum extra volume out of a fixed budget — no guesswork.
Every dot is a Western outlet. Spend rises with untapped headroom — colour shows competition. History is not in the picture.
