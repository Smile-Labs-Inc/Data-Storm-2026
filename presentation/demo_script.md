# Outlet Intelligence — Demo Script (≤ 2 min)

Total speaking time ≈ 1:50.
Format: **[DO]** = what to click/show on screen · **🎙️ SAY** = read aloud.

---

### 0:00 – 0:12 · Intro + the one number

**[DO]** Open the app on the **🏠 Executive Dashboard** tab so the **hero ROI banner** is the first thing on screen.

🎙️ *"Meet Outlet Intelligence by smile Labs. The headline: a LKR 4.9 million promotional plan that returns 27 million in revenue — a five-and-a-half-times return — across 20,000 Sri Lankan beverage outlets. Here's how we get there."*

---

### 0:12 – 0:32 · Executive Dashboard

**[DO]** Move the cursor across the **province KPI cards**, then the **headroom bar chart**, then the **map**.

🎙️ *"Leaders see every province's untapped potential at a glance. Western holds the deepest headroom; the chart ranks which outlet types to prioritise; and the map plots all 20,000 outlets, colour-coded by growth opportunity."*

**[DO]** Scroll to **AI Digest**, click **"Refresh with AI ✨"**, let the bullets generate.

🎙️ *"And our AI writes the executive summary for you — live, grounded in the real numbers."*

---

### 0:32 – 0:47 · Model Credibility (the trust beat)

**[DO]** Click the **🎯 Model Credibility** tab. Hover the four metric cards, then the **prediction-interval chart**.

🎙️ *"But a number is only as good as the model behind it. Every prediction passes a six-point validation suite, ships with calibrated confidence intervals, and sits inside worst-case bounds — 99.98% of the time. This isn't a black box; it's auditable."*

---

### 0:47 – 1:12 · Hermes (the wow moment)

**[DO]** Click **⚡ Hermes** in the sidebar. Click the chip **"Which province has the most untapped headroom?"** — let it stream in silence for ~2 seconds.

🎙️ *"This is Hermes, our conversational analyst. Ask anything in plain English…"*

**[DO]** Type a free-form question: **"Summarise the top 5 investment opportunities."** Let it stream.

🎙️ *"…and it answers instantly, always grounded in the data — never guessing. A market analyst on call."*

---

### 1:12 – 1:32 · Scenario Planner

**[DO]** Click **📈 Scenario Planner**. Drag the **budget slider** left to right and back; point at the **bend** in the curve.

🎙️ *"Planning a budget? Drag the slider and watch the projected impact update — outlets funded, volume, revenue. And notice the curve bends: this is the optimiser's real diminishing-returns frontier, so the sixth million rupee buys less than the first. Honest economics, not a straight line."*

---

### 1:32 – 1:50 · Drill-down + close

**[DO]** Click **🔍 Outlet drill-down**, pick a top outlet, click **"✨ Generate explanation"**.

🎙️ *"And for any single outlet, our explainability engine reveals why the model scored it — and the exact spend we recommend. From boardroom to single store, validated end to end. Outlet Intelligence turns data into decisions. Thank you."*

## Recording tips

- **Pre-load before recording:** paste your Anthropic API key in the sidebar first so Hermes streams live and the AI digest works. Do a dry run of "Refresh with AI" and one Hermes chip to learn the latency.
- **Lead with the number:** the 5.5× ROI banner is your hook — make sure it's visible at second zero.
- **Pace the streaming:** the text-stream effect is your best visual — let one Hermes answer type out in silence for ~2 seconds; don't talk over the whole thing.
- **Point at the bend:** on the Scenario Planner, physically gesture at where the curve flattens — it's the proof your model is real, not linear.
- **Cursor = pointer:** move slowly and deliberately; hovering cards reads better than fast clicks.
- **Fallback safety:** if the network is shaky, Hermes still answers offline from the data — it won't break on camera.
- **One browser tab, full screen, dark theme on** (already configured) — looks sharp in the recording.
