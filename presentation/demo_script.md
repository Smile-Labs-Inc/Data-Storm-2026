# Outlet Intelligence — Demo Script (≤ 2 min)

Total speaking time ≈ 1:50.
Format: **[DO]** = what to click/show on screen · **🎙️ SAY** = read aloud.

---

### 0:00 – 0:15 · Intro

**[DO]** Open the app on the **🏠 Executive Dashboard** tab. Sit still for a beat.

🎙️ *"Meet Outlet Intelligence by smile Labs — a decision-support platform that turns 20,000 Sri Lankan beverage outlets into a clear growth plan. We've built the full stack: a machine-learning potential model, an explainability layer, and a budget optimizer — all wrapped in one interactive app."*

---

### 0:15 – 0:40 · Executive Dashboard

**[DO]** Slowly move the cursor across the **province KPI cards**, then the **headroom bar chart**, then the **map**.

🎙️ *"At a glance, leaders see every province's untapped potential. Western alone holds the deepest headroom. The chart ranks which outlet types to prioritise, and the map plots all 20,000 outlets, colour-coded by their growth opportunity."*

**[DO]** Scroll to **AI Digest**, click **"Refresh with AI ✨"**, let the bullets generate.

🎙️ *"And our AI digest writes the executive summary for you — live, grounded in the real numbers."*

---

### 0:40 – 1:10 · Hermes (the wow moment)

**[DO]** Click **⚡ Hermes** in the sidebar. Click the chip **"Which province has the most untapped headroom?"** — let it stream.

🎙️ *"This is Hermes, our conversational analyst. Ask anything in plain English…"*

**[DO]** As text streams, then type a free-form question: **"Summarise the top 5 investment opportunities."** Let it stream.

🎙️ *"…and it answers instantly, always grounded in the outlet data — never guessing. It's like having a market analyst on call."*

---

### 1:10 – 1:35 · Scenario Planner

**[DO]** Click **📈 Scenario Planner**. Drag the **budget slider** from left to right and back.

🎙️ *"Planning a budget? Drag the slider and watch the projected impact update in real time — outlets funded, incremental volume, and revenue uplift. The curve shows exactly what every extra rupee buys."*

---

### 1:35 – 1:50 · Drill-down + close

**[DO]** Click **🔍 Outlet drill-down**, pick a top outlet, click **"✨ Generate explanation"**.

🎙️ *"And for any single outlet, our explainability engine reveals why the model scored it — and the exact spend we recommend. From boardroom to single store, Outlet Intelligence turns data into decisions. Thank you."*

---

## Recording tips

- **Pre-load before recording:** paste your Anthropic API key in the sidebar first so Hermes streams live and the AI digest works. Do a dry run of the "Refresh with AI" and one Hermes chip so you know the latency.
- **Pace the streaming:** the text-stream effect is your best visual — don't talk over the whole thing; let one answer visibly type out in silence for ~2 seconds.
- **Cursor = pointer:** move slowly and deliberately; hovering the KPI cards reads better on video than fast clicks.
- **Fallback safety:** if the network is shaky, Hermes still answers offline from the data — it won't break on camera.
- **One browser tab, full screen, dark theme on** (already configured) — looks sharp in the recording.
